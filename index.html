Account Deletion – Prombot

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Delete in the App

  1. Sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Account → Manage Account → Delete Account.
  3. Read the warning, type DELETE, and confirm.
  4. Complete any required recent-sign-in verification.

Deletion is permanent. It removes your Firebase sign-in account and associated app profile, tools, installed-tool references, saved response history, and associated files through the app's deletion process. Local app data is cleared as part of that process.

Request Deletion Without the App

Email aitoolcreator84@gmail.com to request deletion of your Prombot account or specific personal data. Identify the account using its sign-in email and state whether you want the entire account deleted or specify the data concerned. Do not send your password, verification codes, payment-card information, or unnecessary sensitive content. We may ask you to verify ownership before acting.

Provider Cleanup and Retention

Our backend queues additional cleanup after Firebase account deletion, including RevenueCat customer deletion and a Google Analytics (GA4) deletion request using the account's User-ID. This runs asynchronously with retries; returning to the welcome screen does not mean every provider has completed erasure.

Some usage, security, fraud-prevention, diagnostic, transaction, backup, and provider-held records may be retained where needed for legitimate operational or legal purposes. Retention depends on the record type, purpose, legal obligations, and provider deletion processes. We do not promise a universal 30-day deletion deadline. Contact us for information about a specific request.

Public tools may already have been added or copied by other users; independent copies and records held by Apple or Google are not necessarily removed by deleting your Prombot account. See our Privacy Policy for more information.

Subscriptions and Analytics Choices

Account deletion does not cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel it through the store account used to purchase it to avoid future renewal charges. Uninstalling Prombot does not delete your account or cancel a subscription.

If you only want to stop optional usage Analytics, switch off Account → Manage Account → Share app usage data. That stops new optional collection through our integration and revokes the subscription Analytics association; it is not a request to erase historical data. You can email us to request deletion of previously collected personal information.
